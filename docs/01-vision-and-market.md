# 01 — Vision & Market

## Business model

seguro.com.py is a **lead generation and comparison platform** for insurance
in Paraguay. The playbook is proven by Swedish comparison sites (Insplanet,
Compricer, Zmarta): own the customer's search intent, capture a qualified
lead through a great funnel, and sell that lead to the partner best placed to
close it.

Revenue models, in order of implementation:

1. **CPL (cost per lead)** — partner pays a fixed price per validated lead.
   Simplest to start with; requires lead-quality trust.
2. **Revenue share / CPA** — partner pays per issued policy. Higher value,
   requires conversion feedback from the partner (API or monthly report).
3. **Exclusive vs. shared leads** — exclusive leads (one partner) command
   3–5× the price of shared leads (sent to up to 3 partners).
4. **Featured placement / sponsorship** — later, once organic traffic exists.

## Why Paraguay

- **Very low insurance penetration** (~1 % of GDP, among the lowest in Latin
  America) — the market is early, which means low competition for digital
  demand capture and a long growth runway.
- **Cheap clicks**: Google Ads CPCs for insurance terms in Paraguay are a
  fraction of European/US prices, while lead values to insurers are
  meaningful.
- **No dominant local comparison player** — the "Compricer of Paraguay"
  position is open.
- **Mobile + WhatsApp culture**: nearly all traffic will be mobile and users
  strongly prefer WhatsApp over forms and email. This is a product design
  constraint *and* a conversion advantage (see doc 07).

## Partner landscape (initial target list)

Insurers regulated by the Superintendencia de Seguros (Banco Central del
Paraguay). Realistic first partners are the digitally active ones and,
importantly, **brokers (corredores de seguros)** who are hungrier for leads
than the insurers themselves:

- MAPFRE Paraguay, La Consolidada, Aseguradora del Este, Patria Seguros,
  Seguridad Seguros, Aseguradora Tajy, Regional Seguros, Grupo General.
- Prepaid medicine: Asismed, Santa Clara, Medicina Prepagada San Lucas.
- Independent brokers in Asunción / Ciudad del Este / Encarnación.

**Strategy note:** start with 2–3 brokers as partners (fast yes, they handle
any insurer), then move up to direct insurer deals once volume proves out.

## Positioning & brand

- Name is the category: *seguro* = insurance. The exact-match domain is the
  moat — build the brand as "el comparador de seguros del Paraguay".
- Tone: simple, trustworthy, local. Prices in guaraníes (₲), local examples
  (Asunción traffic, motos, chipa-level everyday language), Spanish only at
  launch (Guaraní phrases in marketing copy where it adds warmth).
- Trust signals matter more in a low-trust market: partner logos, regulator
  mention, real reviews, WhatsApp contact, no "hidden costs" messaging.
