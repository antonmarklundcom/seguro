## 1. Model

An information site for SEO and organic traffic. We collect no consumer data,
sell no policy, send no lead, run no ads. Revenue: none for now. Costs: domain
and hosting (paid), the owner's time, optional one-hour legal review. The
point of this phase is to earn trust and traffic, then decide on money.

## 4. Ethics and brand

- **Will publish:** coverage and exclusions explained neutrally; legal status
  with source and date; how to verify a provider; how to claim; "qué mirar antes
  de contratar".
- **Will not publish:** "el mejor", rankings, price promises, fear or urgency
  copy, insurer logos, advice on which insurer to pick, health questions.
- **Independence:** we owe nobody. If that ever changes, we say so on the page
  (`DISCLAIMERS.md` §10) and the order stays alphabetical.
- **Errors:** a corrections mailbox; dated updates; a visible "last reviewed".

## 5. What later monetisation would need legally (not now)

- **Display ads (AdSense):** Paraguay listed as available (snippet); insurance
  publisher limits unverified; tax treatment of the foreign income unverified.
- **Sponsored content or listing fees from insurers or brokers:** disclose;
  keep alphabetical order; ask the lawyer whether paid listing is "promoción"
  under Res. SS.SG. 102/08 (unverified).
- **Affiliate links or lead referral:** whether this is "intermediación" under
  Ley 827/96 Art. 70 is untested. Art. 82 says non-registered persons get no
  commission for intermediation, so **per-policy pay is the dangerous
  structure**; per-lead fixed pay and a regulated corredor as the sales party
  are the safer ones. Ask the lawyer first. Old analysis:
  `LEGAL-AUDIT-LEADGEN-OLD.md`, `BUSINESS-LEADGEN-OLD.md`.
- **Always:** a company (EAS), an accountant (IVA 10%, IRE, SIFEN) and Ley 7593
  compliance before any data-linked revenue.

## 6. Where we are not safe yet

1. **Res. SS.SG. 102/08 and Art. 129** could reach even neutral content that
   reads like an offer or is incomplete. The scan and the disclaimers reduce
   this; only the lawyer can close it.
2. **Stating the SOA/SOAT status wrongly** would spread misinformation; wording
   needs re-verification against official texts.
3. **Naming an unauthorised entity** in a directory. Hence directory = Wave 2,
   verified entries only.
