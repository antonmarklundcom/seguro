# 07 — Opportunities (beyond the base plan)

## 1. WhatsApp-first funnel — the Paraguay unlock
Sweden converts with forms; Paraguay converts with WhatsApp. Build the
funnel so "Cotizar por WhatsApp" is a first-class path: click-to-WhatsApp
with a prefilled message, a bot (Cloud API) that asks the same funnel
questions conversationally, and lead creation from the chat. Competitors
will have forms; we'll have conversations. Also: WhatsApp remarketing to
funnel abandoners (with consent) massively outperforms email in LatAm.

## 2. Indicative price data as content moat
Collect partner rate cards + real quote outcomes into an anonymized price
database → publish "¿Cuánto cuesta el seguro de auto en Paraguay? (actualizado
2026)" with charts by vehicle/city/age. Nobody in PY publishes this. It earns
links, ranks, gets cited by press, and feeds a "precio estimado" widget in the
funnel that lifts conversion.

## 3. Reviews of insurers
`/aseguradoras/*` pages + verified consumer reviews (post-lead follow-up
email: "¿Te atendieron bien?") → `AggregateRating` stars in the SERP, unique
UGC content, and leverage in partner negotiations ("your response time is
public"). This is the Trustpilot-of-insurance-PY position, free.

## 4. The outcome-data flywheel (compounding advantage)
Every partner outcome (accepted/rejected/sold) improves: lead scoring →
routing → OCI bidding → cheaper high-quality leads → better partner results →
more outcome data. After 12 months this loop is very hard for a copycat to
replicate even with more money.

## 5. Embedded/B2B2C distribution
Car dealerships (new car = insurance moment), motorcycle dealers, banks
(loan = life insurance moment), travel agencies (Schengen). A simple
white-label quote widget (`<iframe>`/script) with a `ref=` partner code
turns them into free lead sources on rev-share.

## 6. Guaraní-flavored brand marketing
Spanish for SEO, but marketing creative with Guaraní warmth ("¡Anike
reñeñandu vai! — estás asegurado") differentiates the brand emotionally in a
way foreign-feeling insurers can't copy. Cheap, memorable, very PY.

## 7. Portfolio of exact-match .com.py domains
The platform (doc 09) makes each new domain ~a config file + content:
`prestamo.com.py` (loans — highest lead values), plus obvious candidates to
register/acquire now while cheap: internet/telefonia, mudanza, abogado,
contador, energia-solar. Shared lead engine, shared partner-billing, shared
tracking. This is the Zmarta/Lendo group model applied to an open market.

## 8. Programmatic long-tail SEO (phase 2, carefully)
Vehicle-model pages (`/seguro-de-auto/toyota-hilux/`) generated from a
vehicle DB + real quote stats per model. Only ship once each page has real
data (price ranges per model) so it's genuinely useful — then it's hundreds
of zero-competition rankings.

## 9. Email/WhatsApp nurture on renewals
Insurance renews yearly: a lead that didn't convert today is a lead again in
11 months. Capture renewal month in the funnel ("¿Cuándo vence tu seguro
actual?") → automated re-engagement. LTV per captured contact multiplies
without new ad spend.

## 10. Data products for partners (later)
Aggregated demand analytics ("searches for seguro de moto in Encarnación grew
40 %") as a paid dashboard or QBR artifact — makes us look like a market
authority and deepens partner lock-in.
