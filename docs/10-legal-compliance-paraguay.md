# 10 — Legal & Compliance: Operating as an Unlicensed Marketing Partner in Paraguay

> Research compiled 2026-08-21 (Fable 5, web-sourced). **This is research,
> not legal advice.** Article numbers marked *(verify)* come from
> search-extracts and law-firm commentary, not the official PDFs — local
> counsel must confirm them (owner task ⚖️L1 in PLAN.md).
> Implementation sessions: §3 (the line) and §10 (PR mapping) are the parts
> that constrain code and copy.

## 1. The operating model in one paragraph

seguro.com.py operates as a **marketing / lead-referral company**, never an
insurance intermediary. It educates, advertises, captures interest +
contact data with explicit logged consent, and sells those leads for a
**fixed per-lead fee** to corredores and insurers matriculated with the
Superintendencia de Seguros (SIS/BCP). It never advises, never recommends a
specific insurer, never generates or transmits quotes/proposals, never
touches premium money, and never takes premium- or commission-linked
remuneration. Data handling is built to Ley 7593/2025 standards from day
one even though full force arrives ~Nov 2027.

## 2. Insurance regulation — Ley 827/96 and the Superintendencia de Seguros

- **Ley 827/96 "De Seguros"** creates the SIS (inside Banco Central del
  Paraguay) as Autoridad de Control. Official text:
  [BCP PDF](https://www.bcp.gov.py/documents/20117/213083/LEY_827_96_DE_SEGUROS.pdf/68f0897c-3e19-22c3-4904-367b1b2937a9?t=1741806943153) ·
  [BACN](https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/703/de-seguros).
- An **agente/corredor de seguros** is whoever "intermedia en la
  contratación de seguros" *(≈Art. 69, verify)*; **intermediación may only
  be exercised by matriculated agents/corredores** *(≈Art. 70, verify)*.
- **≈Art. 76 (verify):** the intermediary presents the insurance proposal
  in writing under their signature — handling proposals is licensed work.
- **≈Art. 82 (verify) — the pricing-critical article:** unregistered
  persons "no tendrán derecho a percibir comisión alguna" for
  intermediation. A premium-percentage payment to us would (a) invite the
  SIS to recharacterize the business as unlicensed intermediation
  (sanctions ≈Arts. 109–116, fines up to ~1,000 minimum daily wages,
  verify) and (b) likely be **unenforceable** — a partner could refuse to
  pay and we'd have no legal claim.
- **Res. SS.SG. 210 (25 Sep 2025)** regulates insurance sales through
  digital/non-presential channels — but it is addressed to *regulated*
  entities (insurers, agentes, corredores). It neither licenses nor frees
  comparators/lead generators. It does mean the actual digital *sale* flow
  (e-signature on proposals, pre-contractual info, call recording) belongs
  on the licensed partner's systems, not ours.
  ([Vouga summary](https://www.vouga.com.py/la-superintendencia-de-seguros-regula-la-comercializacion-de-seguros-por-medios-electronicos-y-canales-no-presenciales/))
- **Res. SS.SG. 031/2026 (30 Jan 2026)** overhauled matriculación:
  competence exams, bond/liability policy, and **non-resident foreigners
  cannot register** as auxiliaries. So "just get licensed" is not a
  fallback without Paraguayan residency.
  ([BCP PDF](https://www.bcp.gov.py/documents/20117/753661/Resoluci%C3%B3n+SS.SG.+N%C2%B0+031_2026.pdf/3e00fd0b-9f62-b3e5-4797-da5b5218224e?t=1770118492670) ·
  [Vouga](https://www.vouga.com.py/actualizacion-del-regimen-de-matriculacion-de-auxiliares-de-seguros-en-paraguay/))
- **No SIS resolution or published enforcement** specifically addresses
  lead generation or comparadores. The lead-gen carve-out is a functional
  interpretation (marketing happens *before* "la contratación"), widely
  used but not settled text → lawyer question Q1 in §9.

## 3. The line — what the site may and may not do

### Allowed (defensible as publicidad/marketing)

- Educational content about insurance types, "why insure", guías.
- Advertising identified, licensed partners' products (like any ad agency).
- Capturing name, contact, product interest and basic risk data (car
  brand/year, city) and passing it to a matriculated corredor/insurer who
  quotes, advises and contracts.
- Indicative price **ranges** clearly labeled as estimates **provided and
  approved by the identified licensed partner**.
- The standing disclosure (footer, every page + consent flow):
  > *"seguro.com.py es un servicio de publicidad y referencia. No somos
  > agentes ni corredores de seguros, no intermediamos en la contratación
  > de seguros ni brindamos asesoramiento. Conectamos personas interesadas
  > con corredores y aseguradoras habilitados por la Superintendencia de
  > Seguros (BCP)."*

### Prohibited — build sessions check every page against this list

- Personalized or binding **quotes generated by us**; any "contratá acá" /
  checkout-like flow; transmitting a proposal to an insurer.
- **Advice/recommendation language.** Never: "te asesoramos",
  "recomendamos", "el mejor seguro para vos es…", "elegimos por vos",
  "nuestros expertos te aconsejan". Allowed instead: "compará", "recibí
  cotizaciones", "te contactan corredores habilitados", "información para
  decidir".
- Superiority-asserting comparisons ("la mejor aseguradora de Paraguay") —
  Ley 1334/98 Art. 36 restricts comparative advertising built on general,
  indiscriminate claims. Objective, sourced, partner-approved facts only.
- Price promises that differ at contracting (Art. 35 publicidad engañosa —
  misleading **including by omission**; advertised terms integrate the
  contract and the burden of proving truthfulness is on the advertiser).
- Receiving premiums or any policy money.
- Collecting **health or credit data** in lead forms (see §4 — sensitive
  data tier of 7593/2025 and Ley 6534/2020 credit-data regime). The
  seguro-medico funnel asks *interest*, never medical conditions.
- Anonymous operation — Ley 4868/2013 (e-commerce) requires the site to
  identify the provider: razón social, RUC, address, contact.

### Uncertain middle (lawyer sign-off before building)

A live multi-insurer price comparator on our domain. Defensible only if
quotes are legally *emitted by the licensed partners* on their systems with
our site as advertising front-end → lawyer question Q3.

## 4. Data protection — the existential rules for a lead seller

- **Ley 7593/2025 "De Protección de Datos Personales"** — comprehensive
  GDPR-style law, promulgated **27 Nov 2025**, **24-month vacatio legis →
  full force ~Nov 2027**; reglamento pending from the Executive (monitor
  MITIC). Applies extraterritorially to anyone offering services to
  residents of Paraguay.
  ([BACN text](https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/12924/ley-n-7593-2025-de-protecci-n-de-datos-personales-en-la-rep-blica-del-paraguay) ·
  [BKM analysis](https://www.berke.com.py/analisis-de-la-ley-n-7593-2025-de-proteccion-de-datos-personales-de-paraguay1/) ·
  [fines overview, ABC Jul 2026](https://www.abc.com.py/negocios/2026/07/23/la-ley-de-datos-multas-de-hasta-10000-jornales-que-ninguna-empresa-puede-ignorar/))
- Today (until ~Nov 2027) non-credit personal data sits in a mostly
  unregulated gap (constitutional habeas data, Art. 135 CN). **We build
  7593-compliant now** — retrofitting a lead database is the expensive path.
- **Ley 6534/2020** governs **credit data** only (in force since 2020,
  repealed old Ley 1682/2001). We never collect credit data, so it stays
  out of scope — keep it that way.

### What 7593/2025 requires of this business

| Obligation | Implementation |
|---|---|
| Legal basis for **selling/transferring leads**: use **explicit consent**, not legitimate interest (balancing test + objection right make LI fragile for data sales) | Unticked checkbox, versioned text naming the transfer: *"Acepto que mis datos sean transferidos a corredores de seguros y aseguradoras habilitados para que me contacten con cotizaciones."* Category of recipients named; partner list linked when partners exist |
| Consent: prior, free, informed, unequivocal, **revocable as easily as given**; **burden of proof on us** | `leads` mirror stores `consent_at`, `consent_text_version`, IP; revocation channel on /privacidad handled like an ARCO request |
| Purpose limitation | Leads used only for insurance-quote contact; no onward resale beyond the consented categories; no repurposing for other verticals without new consent |
| Security (≈Art. 16) + **72 h breach notification** (≈Art. 17) | HTTPS-only, key server-side, PII never in logs, DB access restricted; breach runbook in the owner's ops notes |
| Data-subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, opposition, revocation) — "ARCO" | Contact + procedure published on /privacidad; requests answered from the mirror table |
| Correction duty: notify recipients within **5 business days** when transferred data proves wrong | Clause in the lead purchase agreement (§6) + manual process |
| Sensitive data (health) = stricter tier, fines up to 5,000 jornales (10,000 for minors; general 20–2,500) | Never collect health data; forms 18+ |
| Enforcement: **Agencia Nacional de Protección de Datos Personales** (within MITIC) | Watch for registration/DPO duties in the pending reglamento |

## 5. Consumer & e-commerce law

- **Ley 1334/98 Defensa del Consumidor** — Art. 35 (publicidad engañosa,
  incl. omission), Art. 36 (comparative advertising limits), advertised
  content integrates the contract. Enforced by **SEDECO**, which actively
  sanctions misleading advertising.
  ([BACN](https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/897/ley-n-1334-de-defensa-del-consumidor-y-del-usuario))
- **Ley 4868/2013 Comercio Electrónico** (+ Decreto 1165/2014): provider
  identification on the site (name, RUC, address, contact), clear
  pre-contractual information; fines up to 1,000 jornales.
  ([BACN](https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/961/ley-n-4868-comercio-electronico))

## 6. Marketing communications / anti-spam

- **Ley 5830/2017** (+ Decreto 8000/2017, Res. SEDECO 80/2018): unsolicited
  advertising via **mobile** (calls, SMS, "any messaging via mobile" —
  WhatsApp plausibly included but not named → lawyer Q4) is prohibited
  without documented, express consent. SEDECO's **No Molestar registry**
  ([nomolestar.sedeco.gov.py](https://nomolestar.sedeco.gov.py/)) must be
  respected for any cold outreach; SEDECO has enforced (Claro case,
  Sentencia 495/2024).
- **Email is not covered** by 5830 — governed only by general consumer law
  today and 7593/2025 from Nov 2027. Opt-in + unsubscribe anyway.
- Our own leads are consented contact by design, so 5830 is satisfied for
  them; the rule constrains **cold** partner-recruiting or remarketing to
  mobiles. Default: no cold mobile outreach.

## 7. Entity, tax & invoicing

| Topic | Position |
|---|---|
| Entity | **EAS** (Ley 6480/2020) via eas.mic.gov.py (SUACE). 100% foreign ownership with just a passport; **no minimum capital**; RUC issued by DNIT in the flow; ~3–15 business days; near-zero state fees with proforma bylaws ([MIC](https://www.mic.gov.py/que-son-las-empresas-por-acciones-simplificadas-y-como-crear-una-en-paraguay/) · [FAQ](https://eas.mic.gov.py/Preguntas-frecuentes)) |
| Catch | The **legal representative needs a Paraguayan cédula** — non-resident owner uses a nominee/attorney via apostilled POA until residency |
| Taxes | **IVA 10%** on lead/marketing services invoiced to PY partners; **IRE 10%** (IRE Simple below thresholds). Export-of-services IVA if selling to foreign buyers → lawyer Q6 |
| Invoicing | **SIFEN e-invoicing from day one** — new RUCs must issue electronically (DNIT Res. 21/24 rollout through 2026). Use e-Kuatía'i (free, small taxpayers) or a certified provider; don't plan around paper timbrado ([Avanzia](https://www.avanzialegal.com/blog/factura-electronica-sifen-2026-nuevos-obligados-calendario-dnit)) |

## 8. Lead purchase agreement — clause checklist (owner task ⚖️L4)

1. **Recitals:** we are not a matriculated agente/corredor; we do not
   intermediate, advise, quote or contract; buyer (cite SIS matrícula
   number) performs all intermediation.
2. **Pricing: fixed per-lead fee (₲) or subscription. Never a % of premium
   or commission** (Art. 82, §2 above). Per-*converted*-lead fixed fees are
   a gray middle → lawyer Q2 before offering.
3. **Data protection (7593-ready):** transfer responsable→responsable;
   warranty that logged consent covers the transfer + buyer's contact
   purpose; use limited to quoting/contracting for that person; no onward
   transfer; security; 72 h breach cooperation; ARCO request handling;
   deletion terms; 5-day correction notification.
4. **Communications warranty:** buyer contacts leads within consent scope;
   Ley 5830/No Molestar compliance for any further outreach.
5. **Advertising risk allocation:** prices/claims shown on our site are
   partner-sourced and partner-approved; mutual indemnities (Art. 35/36
   exposure).
6. Lead-quality definition, duplicate/invalid crediting policy, dispute
   window, caps/exclusivity/territory, audit trail access, term,
   Paraguayan law/jurisdiction or arbitration.
